DATA SOURCES

1. Energy Institute, Statistical Review of World Energy 2025
   Official downloads: https://www.energyinst.org/statistical-review/resources-and-data-downloads
   Used for: coal production (Mt, 2014-2024), coal consumption (EJ, 2014-2024),
             selected coal trade by exporter/importer (EJ, 2023-2024).

2. World Bank, Commodity Markets / Pink Sheet
   Official source: https://www.worldbank.org/en/research/commodity-markets
   Used for: Australian thermal coal benchmark price (USD per tonne), 2021-2025 annual
             plus quarterly/monthly detail 2024-Q3 to 2026-01.

FILES
- coal_production_ei_2014_2024.csv     Year, Country, Coal_Production_Mt
- coal_consumption_ei_2014_2024.csv    Year, Country, Coal_Consumption_EJ
- coal_trade_selected_2023_2024.csv    Year, Trade_Role, Country, Coal_Trade_EJ, Share_of_World_Trade_pct
- coal_price_worldbank_2021_2025.csv   Year, Australia_Thermal_Coal_Price_USD_per_mt
- coal_price_detail_worldbank.csv      Period (quarters "YYYY-Qn" AND months "YYYY-MM" mixed), Price_USD_per_mt

KNOWN DATA ISSUES (flagged automatically by python/market_analysis.py, see data/processed/data_quality_report.csv)
- Indonesia consumption rises 59.3% in 2023 (2.75 -> 4.38 EJ). Verify against the Energy Institute workbook.
- Russian Federation appears as an exporter in 2023 but not in 2024. Like-for-like comparisons exclude it.
- Price history (2021-2025) is shorter than production/consumption history (2014-2024).
- Japan production is about 0.5 Mt, so its percentage changes are not meaningful.
- Trade covers selected countries only; "share of world trade" uses the Energy Institute's world total.

REFRESHING THE DATA
Download the Energy Institute and Pink Sheet workbooks from the links above, copy the relevant columns into the CSV
files in this folder (keep the same headers), then run: python python/market_analysis.py
